# Colour Matching Guide

## Neutral colours

Black, white, grey, navy, beige, tan, and olive are neutrals. Neutrals combine
with almost everything, so an outfit built mostly from neutrals is hard to get
wrong. A good rule is to keep most of the outfit neutral and add at most one
stronger colour or pattern.

## Colours that go with navy

Navy pairs well with white, grey, beige, tan, olive, and brown. Navy with tan
or brown leather is a classic smart casual combination. Navy and black can be
worn together when the difference in shade is clear, but the pairing looks
best with a light colour, such as a white shirt, between them.

## Black and brown

Black and brown can be worn together when they are clearly different shades,
for example dark jeans with brown boots. For more formal outfits, keep shoes
and belt in the same colour family: brown shoes with a brown belt, black shoes
with a black belt.

## Denim

Blue denim acts as a neutral. Dark jeans pair with grey, white, black, olive,
navy, and earth tones. Wearing a denim jacket with jeans works best when the
two washes differ clearly in shade.

## Patterns

Wear one pattern at a time, such as a striped scarf or a plaid flannel shirt,
and keep the rest plain. Pick the other colours from the colours in the
pattern so the outfit looks planned.
